✦ Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

How do I choose the right system size?

Your electricity usage, daytime consumption, roof space and shading all matter. A site assessment helps confirm the right system.

Is the EMI calculator exact?

No. It is a planning estimate. The lender's interest rate, fees and eligibility rules can change your final EMI.

Are government subsidies included?

No. Sample prices and calculations exclude subsidies. Check current official scheme rules and eligibility before making a decision.

How do I get a final quotation?

Send an enquiry so the team can discuss your property, equipment preferences and installation requirements.
